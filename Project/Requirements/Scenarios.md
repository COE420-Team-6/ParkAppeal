# Scenarios

| Scenario ID | Scenario Title | Actor/Stakeholder | Scenario Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S-01 | Appeal submitted within 14-day window | Permit Holder | Ahmed receives a 200 AED citation for "no valid permit". On day 12, Ahmed logs in and opens the citation from his citations list. Ahmed selects "Appeal", and the system verifies that the citation exists and is still within the 14-day window. Ahmed enters the following reason: "permit was displayed but was not properly captured". Ahmed attaches a 3MB photo of the permit. Ahmed clicks "Submit Appeal", and the system puts the citation on hold. Ahmed receives an email confirming his appeal was successfully sent. A few days later, Ahmed checks his appeals and sees that it is "Under Review". An appeals officer reviews the citation and photo and approves the appeal. Within a week of submission, Ahmed receives confirmation that his appeal was successful and that the citation was waived. |
| S-02 | Appeal submitted outside of the 14-day window | Permit Holder | Sara receives a 100 AED citation for "unauthorized parking at a roundabout". On day 16, Sara logs in and opens the citation from her citations list. Sara selects "Appeal", but the system rejects the request and shows an error message stating: "Appeal window has passed". |
| S-03 | Permit application using mobile device | Applicant | Ali opens ParkAppeal on his phone using a web browser. The system adjusts the page layout to fit the mobile screen. Ali logs in and selects "Apply for Permit." He enters his vehicle information and selects the type of parking permit. Ali uploads a photo of the required document from his phone. The system checks that all required fields are completed. Ali presses "Submit Application." The system displays a confirmation message and shows the application status as "Pending Review." Ali can return to the dashboard later and easily view the status of his application. |
| S-04 | Issuing a violation for an expired permit | Parking Officer | Officer Ahmed begins his morning patrol of Lot C at 09:15. Ahmed notices vehicle SHJ-48213 parked in a staff permit zone. Ahmed logs into ParkAppeal on his phone and searches the plate SHJ-48213. Ahmed sees that the vehicle holds staff permit P-2026-0412, which expired on 31st August 2026. Ahmed selects the violation type "Expired permit" and the system fills in the vehicle and permit details. Ahmed photographs the licence plate and the zone sign and attaches both images to the record. Ahmed submits the violation and the system creates record V-2026-1187, stamped 27th September 2026 09:17. The permit holder receives a notification that a violation has been issued against their vehicle. |
| S-05 | Issuing a violation to a vehicle with no permit on record | Parking Officer | Officer Ahmed finds vehicle DXB-90115 parked in a reserved visitor bay at 13:40. Ahmed searches the plate DXB-90115 in ParkAppeal. Ahmed discovers that the plate matches no registered vehicle and no permit. Ahmed selects the violation type "No valid permit" and enters bay number B-22. Ahmed photographs the vehicle and attaches the image to the record. Ahmed submits the violation and the system creates the record without a permit or account link. The system marks the record as unmatched and places it in the administrator's review queue. |
| S-06 | Issue an approved parking permit within quota | Parking Administrator | An applicant submits a student permit request for vehicle DXB-76220. The configured student eligibility checks pass and the request enters Pending Review. An administrator reviews the result and approves the application using UC-07. ParkAppeal verifies that the student permit quota still has capacity, creates exactly one permit linked to the approved application and vehicle, assigns a unique permit number and configured start and expiry dates, and shows the issued permit as Active. If quota has been filled before issuance, the system blocks issuance and shows the administrator why. When the expiry date passes, the permit status becomes Expired and appears in the expiry list. The quota and validity period are prototype configuration values, not asserted AUS policy. |
