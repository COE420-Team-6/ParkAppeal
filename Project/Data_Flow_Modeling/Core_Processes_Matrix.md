# Core Processes Matrix

Processes are the five major processes of the Level-0 DFD (Figure 2). Data names are the exact flow names used in the Level-0 DFD. FR, UC, NFR references point to `Project/Requirements/`.

| Process No. and Name | Input Data | Output Data | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1.0 Manage Permit Applications** | Permit Application Details (from Permit Holder); Permit Approval Decision and Eligibility Criteria and Quota Settings (from Administrator); Applicant and Vehicle Details (from D1); Existing Permit Record and Eligibility and Quota Rules (from D2) | New Permit Record and Eligibility and Quota Rules (to D2); Pending Application List (to Administrator); Permit Status Change (to 5.0) | 1. The Permit Holder must enter vehicle details, select a permit type and upload the required documents (from any device) before an application can be submitted (FR-08, UC-06).<br>2. The Administrator sets the eligibility criteria and the active-permit quota of each permit type, stored in D2 (FR-16, FR-17). Each application is evaluated against the criteria of the selected permit type; the result and any unmet criteria are shown to the Administrator (FR-18).<br>3. Only the Administrator approves or rejects an application. Approval of an eligible application issues exactly one permit, only when the permit type still has quota, with a unique permit number, start date, expiry date and Active status (FR-17, FR-19, UC-19).<br>4. A permit is renewed before it expires; a permit past its expiry date is shown as Expired (UC-08, FR-20).<br>5. Every approval, rejection, issuance or renewal produces a Permit Status Change for notification. |
| **2.0 Manage Parking Violations** | Licence Plate Enquiry and Violation Details and Evidence (from Parking Officer); Violation Void Request (from Administrator); Registered Vehicle Record (from D1); Permit Validity Status (from D2); Existing Violation Record (from D3) | Vehicle and Permit Status (to Parking Officer); New Violation Record (to D3); Unmatched Violation List (to Administrator); Violation Issued Event (to 5.0) | 1. Only an authenticated Parking Officer can look up a plate and record a violation; the plate lookup is performed every time before a violation is recorded (FR-11, UC-12 includes UC-11).<br>2. A violation requires a type chosen from a predefined list and a location; the system stamps the date and time of issue (FR-12).<br>3. Up to 5 photographs (JPG or PNG, max 5 MB each) can be attached at the time of issue (FR-13).<br>4. If the plate matches no registered vehicle, the record is still created without a permit or account link and marked "unmatched" for Administrator review (FR-14).<br>5. An issued violation cannot be edited or deleted; only an Administrator can void it, with a written reason, and the original record is kept (FR-15, UC-15). |
| **3.0 Process Appeals** | Appeal Submission and Evidence (from Permit Holder); Appeal Decision and Justification (from Appeals Officer); Violation Record and Evidence (from D3); Appeal Status (from D4) | Appeal Details and Evidence (to Appeals Officer); Appeal Record and Decision (to D4); Violation Void Status (to D3); Appeal Status Change (to 5.0) | 1. An appeal must match an existing violation record, which is verified at submission (UC-01 includes Verify Violation Record).<br>2. An appeal is accepted only within 14 days of the citation issue date; later attempts are refused with "Appeal window has passed" (FR-01, FR-02).<br>3. An appeal carries a written reason and up to 3 attachments (PDF or JPG, max 5 MB each) (FR-01).<br>4. The Appeals Officer reviews the violation record and evidence and records a decision (approve, reduce or reject) with a written justification (FR-04, UC-04).<br>5. Each decision is logged with a timestamp and user ID, and only authenticated Appeals Officers can decide (NFR-02).<br>6. The Permit Holder can view the status of any appeal they submitted (FR-03). When an appeal is approved, the violation's void status is updated in D3 (Violation Void Status). |
| **4.0 Search and Report** | Search and Report Query (from Administrator); Permit Data (from D2); Violation Data (from D3); Appeal Data (from D4) | Search Results and Reports (to Administrator) | 1. Search and filtering of permits, violations and appeals is by status or date (FR-09, UC-09).<br>2. Only the Administrator can run searches and reports.<br>3. Reports cover permits issued, violations recorded and appeal outcomes (Scope.md, Section 3).<br>4. Permits past their expiry date appear as Expired in results (FR-20).<br>5. The process is read-only: it never changes a record in D2, D3 or D4. |
| **5.0 Manage Notifications** | Permit Status Change (from 1.0); Violation Issued Event (from 2.0); Appeal Status Change (from 3.0); Account Contact Details (from D1) | Status Notification (to Permit Holder) | 1. A notification is sent whenever a permit application, violation or appeal changes status (FR-10, UC-10).<br>2. A confirmation email is sent when an appeal is submitted (FR-01).<br>3. Channels are limited to in-app and email; SMS and push are out of scope (Scope.md, Section 4).<br>4. Contact details are always read from the account record in D1, never entered again by staff. |
