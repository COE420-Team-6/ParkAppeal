## Problem
Parking permits and violation appeals at large campuses and organisations are
often handled through paper forms, email threads, and spreadsheets. Applicants
have no visibility into the status of a request, staff process the same
information manually more than once, and appeals are difficult to track from
submission to final decision. ParkAppeal replaces this with a single system for
issuing permits and managing appeals.

## Intended Users
- **Applicants** (students, staff, and visitors) who apply for parking permits
  and contest violations.
- **Parking officers** who issue violations and review evidence.
- **Administrators** who set permit quotas, review appeals, and issue decisions.

## Main Features
- Online permit application with document upload and eligibility checking.
- Permit issuance, renewal, and expiry tracking.
- Violation records linked to vehicles and permits.
- Appeal submission with supporting evidence and a defined review workflow.
- Status tracking and notifications for applicants.
- Administrative dashboard and reporting.

## Key Functionalities
- Account registration and role-based access for applicants, officers,
  and administrators.
- Submitting, reviewing, approving, or rejecting permit applications.
- Recording violations and linking them to registered vehicles.
- Filing an appeal within a set time window, with evidence attachments.
- Routing appeals to a reviewer, recording the decision and its justification,
  and notifying the applicant.
- Searching and filtering permits, violations, and appeals by date, status,
  or user.
- Generating reports on permits issued, violations recorded, and appeal outcomes.
